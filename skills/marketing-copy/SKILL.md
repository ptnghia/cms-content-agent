# Marketing copy

1. Identify: who buys, what they want, why they should choose us (differentiators **that are in the data**). If there are none, ask, or write at a general level and add a note.
2. Structure: a headline stating the benefit → 2–3 specific strengths (benefits first, features second) → real evidence (statistics, reviews that are in the data) → a clear CTA with one action.
3. Tone follows `knowledge/style-guide.md`; short, active sentences; avoid empty clichés ("leading", "number 1 trusted") unless there is evidence for them.
4. **Never invent** prices, promotions, deadlines, policies, quantities or reviews. Do not add or change price figures.
5. Other channels (social media, email): write drafts to `drafts/` (do not send via `outbox/`). Email: 3 subject line options (≤ 60 characters) + preheader + short body + 1 CTA. Social media: 2 versions of different lengths + suggested relevant hashtags.
6. SEO for sales pages still follows `seo-onpage`; do not let keywords make sentences awkward.
